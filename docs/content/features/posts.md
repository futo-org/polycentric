# Posts

A post is up to 2000 characters of text. You can attach up to four images
from your photo library or camera, and the first link you type unfurls into
a preview card (remove it with the X if you don't want it).

Posts are published to every server your identity uses, signed by your keys.
No single server can alter them or take them away from your audience.

## Replies and quotes

Every post can be replied to, and threads keep the conversation together.
You can also quote a post to share it with your own comment on top.

## Posts from other apps

Polycentric is an open network, so posts can come from applications other
than Harbor. When they do, the post shows "Posted with" and the name of the
app above the text. If the app publishes a website, the name links to it.

## Mentions and links

URLs in a post are tappable. Mention someone by typing their alias
(`@user@domain.com`) or their full identity key; mentions link to their
profile.

## Deleting a post

Open the "…" menu on your own post and choose delete. Clients stop showing
the post. Posts can't be edited after publishing, so delete and repost if
you need to change something.
